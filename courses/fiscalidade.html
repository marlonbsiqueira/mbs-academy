Tax & Tax Compliance

Most finance professionals deal with tax every day — yet few have a structured understanding of the tax framework that lets them spot risks, challenge classifications and talk confidently with tax teams and auditors. This program closes that gap: a practical tax framework, applied to operational finance and multinational environments.

Program overview

What this program covers.

Tax is often treated as a separate specialism — something handed to the tax department and never questioned. That approach has a cost: finance professionals who do not understand the tax framework make classification errors, miss optimisation opportunities and are exposed in audits. This program does not train tax specialists — it trains finance professionals who understand enough to work well with tax specialists, spot risks and make better decisions.

The content focuses on the Portuguese and European framework, with particular attention to the cross-cutting topics of multinational environments: intra-EU VAT, transfer pricing, group taxation and filing obligations.

The three areas covered
Modules 1–2
VAT — Value Added Tax
General rules, rates, exemptions, intra-EU VAT, deductibility and filing obligations.
Modules 3–4
CIT — Corporate Income Tax (IRC)
Taxable profit computation, tax incentives, deductions, group taxation and the RETGS regime.
Modules 5–6
Compliance & Tax Risk Management
Filing obligations, transfer pricing, tax risk in multinationals and audits.

Who this program is for

📊 Controllers and finance managers who need to understand the tax framework of their operations
🏢 SSC/GBS professionals responsible for multi-jurisdiction operations
🔍 Compliance and internal audit teams who need to test tax-related controls
📋 Financial analysts who want to understand the tax impact on the financial statements
🌍 Finance professionals in multinationals with intra-EU VAT exposure

Program modules

01 VAT — Fundamentals and General Rules ~80 min
How VAT works: scope, charging, deduction and payment to the State
VAT rates in Portugal: standard, intermediate and reduced — what applies where
VAT exemptions: exempt without credit vs. zero-rated, and their impact on deductibility
Place-of-supply rules: when the VAT is Portuguese and when it is not
Filing obligations: periodic VAT return, EC Sales List, VIES
02 Intra-EU VAT and Cross-Border Transactions ~85 min
Intra-EU acquisitions and supplies: rules and mandatory documentation
Cross-border services: the general B2B and B2C rules and their exceptions
Reverse charge: when it applies and how to record it
Imports and exports: import VAT, export exemptions
The most common intra-EU errors and how to avoid them
03 Corporate Tax — Computing Taxable Profit ~90 min
From accounting profit to taxable profit: the logic of the Modelo 22 return
Deductible and non-deductible expenses: the main permanent differences
Interest deductibility limitation (EBITDA rule)
Tax-deductible depreciation and amortisation: rates and rules
Tax losses: carry-forward and current limitations
04 Group Taxation and Tax Incentives ~75 min
RETGS — the Portuguese group taxation regime: how it works
Participation exemption: dividends and capital gains on shareholdings
SIFIDE, RFAI and other investment tax incentives
Taxation of intra-group dividends and interest
Withholding tax: when it applies, rates and filing obligations
05 Transfer Pricing in Multinational Environments ~85 min
The arm's length principle: what it means in practice
Transfer pricing methods: CUP, cost plus, TNMM and others
Documentation obligations: the transfer pricing file and who must prepare it
The most common intra-group transactions: shared services, royalties, financing
Risks and adjustments: what the tax authority checks and how to prepare the company
06 Tax Compliance, Risk and Audit Readiness ~85 min
Annual filing obligations: Modelo 22, IES, DMR and deadlines
SAF-T and what the tax authority can see: implications for how the books are organised
Tax risk mapping: how to identify and prioritise exposures
Preparing for tax inspections: documentation, conduct and managing the process
Building an annual tax calendar and a tax internal-control system
Built from real operational experience
